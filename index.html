Why BlurKit

Three promises. No compromises.

01 · Privacy

Your image never leaves your browser.

Everything runs on your device. No uploads to a server. No cloud processing. No logs. No trail. When you close the tab, there's nothing left behind — not on our end, because there's no "our end."

02 · AI Magic

Faces are found before you click.

The moment your image loads, AI maps every face in the frame. Click one to blur it. Click "Blur all" to redact everyone at once. The model runs locally — fast, accurate, private.

03 · Speed

No signup. No waiting. Ship it.

Open the page, drop your image, blur, download. Under ten seconds, start to finish. No account. No paywall. No "verify your email." Just a tool that does the one thing you came here for.

How it works

Four steps. Zero friction.

01

Drop your image

Drag and drop, or click to select. JPG, PNG, or WebP. Up to 20MB. Loaded entirely in your browser memory.

02

Faces auto-detect

AI scans the image and highlights every face. Click a highlight to blur just that one — or blur everyone with a single button.

03

Blur anything else

Switch to Draw mode and drag a box over license plates, screens, addresses, logos — anything you want redacted.

04

Download the result

Pick your blur style (gaussian, pixelate, redact, or emoji), then hit download. Clean PNG. No watermark.
